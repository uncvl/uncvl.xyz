description_en: |-
  Drone, is a minimalist genre that emphasizes the use of sustained sounds, notes, or tone clusters – called drones. Unlike other genres that use drones as a component (such as to establish tonality), drone music puts drones at the forefront, removing most melody and rhythm.

  A majority of drone music highlights an understated, internally microtonal and primarily timbral sonic movement provided by the acoustics of whichever instrument is creating the drone. In the case of electronic drone, this is often achieved by slight fluctuations in the drones or intentional inharmonicity.

  Drone music has expanded to influence countless other genres, including ambient, drone metal and shoegaze.

Drone’ları bir bileşen olarak kullanan diğer türlerin aksine (örneğin tonaliteyi belirlemek için), drone müziği drone’ları ön plana çıkararak melodi ve ritmi büyük ölçüde ortadan kaldırır.

Elektronik drone söz konusu olduğunda, bu genellikle drone'larda hafif dalgalanmalar veya kasıtlı olarak armoni bozukluğu yaratılarak elde edilir.

description_en: |-
  Industrial hip hop is an experimental hip hop genre that uses industrial music beats and sounds. The production style is generally very dissonant, featuring atonal noise and loud, distorted drums. Vocals are usually more aggressive than typical hip hop and are sometimes shouted. Lyrical content is usually disturbing and/or confrontational, typically involving dystopian or political themes, and other subject matter typical of industrial music.

  Industrial hip hop is inspired by several attempts to combine industrial music with hardcore hip hop and dub. It also anticipates many of the developments of dubstep. Illbient is adjacent to, and possibly a subgenre of, industrial hip hop. Industrial hip hop is connected to the more experimental variants of trip hop. It is crossing over with newly birthed genres such as deconstructed club and trap metal. Contemporary industrial hip hop is also closely connected to digital hardcore and breakcore.

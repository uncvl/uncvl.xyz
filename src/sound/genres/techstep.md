description_en: |-
  Techstep is developed from jungle and hardstep. It usually adheres to drum and bass norms especially in terms of musical structure, with the emphasis on the "drop".

  It is characterized by a dark, sci-fi mood, near-exclusive use of synthesised or sampled sound sources, 2-step kicks and snares and influences from industrial and techno music. It rarely uses instruments that have not been processed by effects. Abstract, synthetic noises, bleeps and squelches are commonly used. Quantized drum-machine kit and percussion sounds are favored over naturalistic human breakbeats. Techstep saw jungle music's obsession with bass change, from aiming for low and deep to exploring timbre, artists aiming to outdo each other with ever more distorted and "twisted" bass sounds.

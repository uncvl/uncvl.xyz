description_en: |-
  Hardstep is a subgenre of drum and bass. It is characterised by a gritty production style that consists of an inner-city feel. The breaks are less choppy than oldschool jungle, and have faster and harder simple electronic melodies. One characteristic is an accentuated, yet sparse percussive beat.

  Most hardstep productions have overly compressed drumming; the atmosphere is mostly minimalistic to have a less melodic and more bassy structure and also uses a signature gritty style of production. The synth bass riffs are made from simple, deep and modulated patterns.

  The genre left strong influences on other drum and bass subgenres such as the more complex techstep and the more simpler-sounding jump-up.

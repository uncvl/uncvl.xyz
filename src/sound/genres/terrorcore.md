description_en: |-
  Terrorcore is faster darker and harsher variant of hardcore often incorporating samples from horror from horror, action and sci-fi movies and and abrasive musical genres such as metal's guitar riffing.

  Having developed alongside speedcore, terrorcore is similarly defined by its high tempos, ranging from around 160 to 300 BPM. The genre retains the distorted kick drums of hardcore and adds additional layers of distortion and other harsh sounds.

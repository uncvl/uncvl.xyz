description_en: |-
  Post-internet (also known as hypnogogic drift) is a branch of vaporwave revolving around computerised abstraction and futuristic, almost dystopian soundscapes.

  This subgenre contains releases that offer aesthetic heavy sampling of sounds and music that are, in essence, a celebration of Internet culture. Much of the music is ambient to the point of being sublimina.

  The thematic focus is less on consumerism and more on the infiltration of digital technology into our lives. Post-internet as a term exists beyond vaporwave but also functions within the genre. Post-internet vaporwave is the uncanny side of the vaporwave. It's the negative of nostalgia, it at once can make you long for the past and also discomfort you.

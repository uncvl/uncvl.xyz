description_en: |-
  Hardcore hip hop refers to hip hop music characterized by confrontation and aggression, whether in the lyricism, vocal delivery, hard beats, heavy production, or any combination thereof.

  Hardcore hip hop is characterised by aggression and confrontation and generally describes violence or anger. Lyrical themes typically include street life and violence, police brutality, drugs, crime, sex, politics.

  Later on, artists experimented further with pushing the boundaries of aggression and intensity by incorporating more noisy and distorted sounds. Industrial hip hop combined hardcore hip hop with industrial music and dub.

description_en: |-
  Raggacore is a style of breakcore that takes the genre’s chaotic and noisy approach to sampling and prominently incorporates samples from ragga and dancehall.

  Raggacore is characterised by ragga voices, a 4/4 rhythm with hard kicks and bass from hardcore, fast and chaotic breakbeats from jungle and breakcore, and an instrumental palette from reggae. Tempo is fast, usually ranging between 180 and 210 BPM.

  Raggacore is sometimes compared to ragga jungle, but being derived from breakcore, raggacore artists use a much more intense and eclectic approach to composition than most jungle producers, and may also include elements of other genres such as gabber and noise.

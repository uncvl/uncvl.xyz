description_en: |-
  Flashcore is a genre that grew out of speedcore and industrial hardcore. It is defined by its complex avant-garde structures, intense wandering soundscapes, scattered rapid short-duration sounds and, usually, layered acousmatic decomposition and influences from IDM and breakcore.

  The genre is generally structured around layered rhythmic staccato-based textures, unintuitive progression with ambient backgrounds. There is also frequent use of laser kicks as well as noise and glitch interplay.

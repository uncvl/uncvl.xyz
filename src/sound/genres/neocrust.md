description_en: |-
  Neocrust is a subgenre of crust punk that incorporates darker, melodic songwriting, straying from traditional crust structures, and takes influences from sludge metal, black metal, screamo, hardcore punk and doom metal. The genre is defined by its central sound being directly informed by a melodic style of crust with dark, brooding atmospheres from metallic influences.

  Unlike most other punk–metal fusion genres, neocrust's sound is neither distinctively rooted in punk or metal, instead frequently shifting between the two, disregarding genre boundaries. It is often dark and heavy however also melodic.

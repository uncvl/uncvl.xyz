description_en: |-
  Emo rap is a fusion genre of hip hop and emo music. The genre fuses characteristics of hip hop music, such as beats and rapping, with the lyrical themes, instrumentals, and vocals commonly found in emo music. Emerging from a background of online underground hip hop, with roots in cloud rap and trap, emo rap became distinct by its blend of various aspects of emo music with the aforementioned genres. Emo rap is further distinguished by provocatively dark lyricism, sullen artist imagery, and a unique mournful, melodic form of delivery.

  It is commonly characterized by moody, melancholic production that combines the atmospheric nature of cloud rap, and trap's typical drum programming, with sampling from alternative rock and emo genres. Sampling often uses 2000s pop punk and emo songs. Emo rap departs from the traditional tones found in modern mainstream hip hop in favor of more emotional and personal lyrical content. Lyrics tend to focus on topics such as depression, loneliness, anxiety, consumption of drugs and alcohol, nihilism, suicide, heartbreak and self-medication.

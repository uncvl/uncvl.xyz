description_en: |-
  Dreambreak blends atmospheric drum‑and‑bass and jungle breakbeats with the abrasive ambient textures.

  Tracks typically deploy chopped amen breaks, granular or buffer‑stutter edits, bitcrushing, and timestretch artifacts under moody synth pads and wistful melodies.

  Compared with classic breakcore, the tempo and editing are often less manic, leaning toward a melancholic, introspective vibe with ambient textures. Glitchbreak is generally more atmospheric, melodic, and slower, with tempos often remaining below 200 BPM. The drum patterns, while complex, are often less frantic than those found in classic breakcore, leading many listeners and artists from the breakcore community to classify the style as a form of atmospheric drum and bass or a modern take on jungle.

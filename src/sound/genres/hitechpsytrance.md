description_en: |-
  Hi-Tech Psytrance, also written as High-Tech or Hitek, is born from the fusion of Full-On and Dark Psy, taking influences from Hardtek.

  Blending elements of darkpsy and full-on psytrance, hi-tech psytrance is most well known for its extreme tempo, which can exceed 200 BPM. It is primarily defined by an exaggerated emphasis on glitchy, jittering sound design as well as complex rolling bassline patterns that often change significantly throughout the course of a track. These elements give hi-tech a dense and chaotic sound even beyond that of its influences.

  The genre is close to Psycore, another genre that fuses Dark Psy with Speedcore. But psycore is more experimental and leans more on atmosphere and is faster.

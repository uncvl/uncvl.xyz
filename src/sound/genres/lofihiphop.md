description_en: |-
  Lo-fi hip hop (also known as chillhop and lo-fi beats) is a form of downtempo music that combines elements of hip hop and chill-out music. Unlike other common definitions of the term, which have to do with equipment quality, the genre's lo-fi sound comes from the warm analogue crackles intentionally added in by producers to emulate the sound of vinyl records and invoke a sense of nostalgia.

  Although lo-fi hip hop is typically instrumental, some artists in the genre would start including rapping in their songs while still maintaining the same production techniques and overall aesthetic.

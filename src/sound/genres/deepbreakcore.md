description_en: |-
  Deep breakcore is a more atmospheric, bass-first strand of breakcore with cinematic or haunted atmospheres between the shocks of the breakbeats.

  While it retains the genre’s hallmark chopped Amen breaks, fast tempos, and heavy distortion, it typically folds in subtler elements — IDM‑like micro‑edits, dub‑inflected space, granular textures, cinematic pads, and ghostly harmonies — to create depth and emotional contour.

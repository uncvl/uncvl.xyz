description_en: |-
  Plugg is a subgenre of trap. Unlike mainline trap, which is defined by bombastic production and rattling hi-hat drum patterns, plugg is dreamy, laidback, atmospheric, spacey, airy, minimal and catchy, having overall lush and jazzy atmosphere with sparse and minimalistic drum and hi hat patterns. The relaxed sound is defined by its common use of subby 808s and heavy low end, airy pads, icy synths, simple chords and repetitive melodies. Songs are commonly lower-tempo between 130 and 145 BPM.

  Drum technique used is polyrhythmic, sparse, bouncy and swinging. Instead of hi-hats in mainline trap, plugg drumming mainly employs beat skips, crash cymbals and punctuated accent snares on half-beats. Productions almost exclusively use claps on third beats, while snares are reduced to only being on half beats. Muffled, heavily low-pass filtered claps are oftentimes used instead of snares. The complete rhythm usually employs several minor variation throughout the song; and it might be turned on and off throughout the song.

  Among the most recognizable traits of plugg are its catchy Nintendo video-game influenced multi-layered melodies with heavy use of chords and xylophone, prevalence of synth pad synths and other soft synthetizer presets.

  Vocally, plugg ranges from instrumental beats without vocals, to songs with either rapping or mellow singing. Plugg performers may employ a variety of vocals styles, including aggressive rapping, mellow rapping, whispering flow, DMV flowand singing. Abundant producer tags are another common element of the style.

  Producers have also very frequently combined the production techniques of the genre with cloud rap.

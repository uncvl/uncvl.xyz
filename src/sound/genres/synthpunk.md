description_en: |-
  Synth punk (also known as electropunk) appropriates the harsh elements of punk rock but replaces the predominance of guitars with synthesizers and drum machines. It typically features fast tempos and aggressive sound samples.

  Synth punk differs from music that may be termed dance-punk in that it is often dissonant and lo-fi, rather than the more upbeat, dance-floor ready feel of dance-punk.

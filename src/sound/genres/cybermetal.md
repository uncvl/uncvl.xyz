description_en: |-
  Cyber metal is a sub-genre of industrial metal. The genre incorporates numerous elements found predominantly in EBM and aggrotech. Characteristics of the genre include the use of more melodic and less repetitive riffs, in opposition to mostly metallic and mechanical sound of industrial. Generally speaking, it utilizes the pummeling guitar sound of thrash music but complements it wish harsh industrial-styled electronics.

  Guitar melodies are played in a similar way to melodic death metal, and keyboards specific to symphonic black metal are frequent as well. Riffs range from thrash-like to groove-like, and often probe the prog metal realm with technical staccato riffs referred to as "jackhammer". The vocals are harsh, ranging from singing techniques typical for aggrotech to black metal shrieks and death metal growls. Lyrical themes range from the heavily political to science-fiction.

  Cyber metal can be easily distinguished from industrial metal because the latter tends to utilize very artificial, digital sounding guitars. While cyber metal does sometimes incorporate more industrial-esque beats ranging from dance to hip-hop in style, as a whole the rhythms are inspired by traditional metal.

description_en: |-
  Chiptune is a subset of electronic music created using the primitive sound synthesis characteristic of early generation home computer & video game hardware. The term is commonly used to refer to tracker format music which intentionally sounds similar to older PSG (programmable sound generator) created music, as well as music that combines PSG sounds with modern musical styles. It has been described as "an interpretation of many genres" since any existing song can be arranged in a chiptune style defined more by choice of instrument and timbre than specific style elements.

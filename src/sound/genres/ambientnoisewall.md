description_en: Ambient noise wall is a form of noise music that combines the static and unchanging approach of harsh noise wall with softer, more approachable textures leaning towards an ambient sound. As with harsh noise wall, ambient noise wall mainly focuses on relentlessly unchanging sound, with little regard towards conventional musicality; however, ambient noise wall artists are more free to explore textural nuances and variation as the music is mostly quieter and less aggressive than typical wall noise. In addition to ambient music, artists may also pull influences from dark ambient, drone and lowercase.

description_en: |-
  IDM is a style of electronic music defined by idiosyncratic experimentation rather than specific genre constraints. It emerged from the culture and sound palette of electronic and rave music styles such as ambient techno, acid house, Detroit techno and breakbeat. IDM is a style characterized by unusual, weird, distorted sounds, and drum lines consisting of very short bleeps and glitches.

  The term "intelligent dance music" has been widely criticised and dismissed by most artists and now used almost exclusively in its acronym form, as the actual meaning came to be interpreted as implying that other styles of dance music are not intelligent. The term is widely reviled but still commonly used. An alternative term 'braindance' is also used.

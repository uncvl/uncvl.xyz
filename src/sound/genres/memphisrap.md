description_en: |-
  Memphis rap (also known as Memphis hip hop) is a style of hardcore hip hop. The style is characterized by its sinister atmosphere, lo-fi nature, fast-paced cowbells, syncopated rhythm pattern, booming sub-bass, and often rapped in a double time flow along with an aggressive delivery. Looping is also a signature with no steadfast rule, although looping is used over chopped edits. Usually, the lyrics are quite dark and depict graphic subject matter.

  Memphis rap is considerably downbeat and rarely indulges in party-oriented songs. Memphis rap's composition is often DIY with a drum machine compared to other hip hop styles that were built on samples. This forced many producers’ hand to experiment with sounds that were unheard-of at the time, this DIY attitude later created a subculture that led to a prominent underground community.

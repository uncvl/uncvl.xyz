description_en: |-
  Harsh noise wall, also known as wall noise or HNW, is an extreme subgenre of noise music. It is a style of harsh noise that features noises layered together to form a static sound, a consistent, unflinching and enveloping wall of monolithic noise.

  The noise present in harsh noise wall is loud and distorted, but differs from other harsh noise in that it does not have much in terms of dynamic changes, instead often taking the form of long compositions that can vary from very slow, subtle change over time to no change whatsoever. Although relatively simple in concept, artists have varied in their approach to the genre. For example, ambient noise wall artists strip back the harshness to embrace more enveloping ambient textures within the harsh noise wall framework.

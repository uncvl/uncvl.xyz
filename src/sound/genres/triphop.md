description_en: |-
  Trip hop (sometimes used synonymously with 'downtempo') is a musical genre that has been described as a psychedelic fusion of hip hop and electronica with slow tempos and an atmospheric sound. Trip hop music fuses several styles and has much in common with other genres; it has several qualities similar to ambient music, its drum-based breakdowns share characteristics with hip-hop and often incorporates elements of jazz, soul, funk, reggae, dub, R&B, and other forms of electronic music.

  Common musical aesthetics include a bass-heavy drumbeat, often providing the slowed down breakbeat samples. Trip hop is also known for its melancholic sound.

description_en: |-
  Future garage is a broad derivative of UK garage. Characteristic sounds are pitched vocal chops, warm filtered reese basses, dark atmospheres (including synth pads, field recordings and other atmospheric sounds) and vinyl crackle. The tempo usually ranges from 130 to 140 BPM.

  Future garage employs soft leads with a round attack or an acoustic lead, subbass or a square wave bass with a modulating filter, and a four-to-the-floor or 2-step garage drum beat with off-the-grid hi-hats. The distinct swing in future garage drum beats is the focus on late or humanized hi-hats between the kick and snare patterns.

  Future garage developed through two distinct waves: One of them with melancholic, gloomy, and hypnotic atmospheres that usually lacked soulful vocals and more upbeat nature, and the other with bright, warm atmospheres and light synth work.

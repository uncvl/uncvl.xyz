description_en: |-
  Hardstyle is a form of hard dance that emerged from hard trance with strong hardcore influences.

  Hardstyle is characterized by its persistent, compressed, repetitive, distorted, four-on-the-floor kick drums, and its use of the reverse bass, and harsh, distorted synths. Melodies are often uplifting and emotional, contrasting with the aggressive beats. Breakdown sections are common, providing contrast to the intense drops. The tempo generally ranges from 140 to 150 beats per minute.

Melodiler genellikle coşkulu ve duygusal olup, agresif ritimlerle tezat oluşturur. Yoğun drop'lara kontrast sağlayan breakdown bölümleri yaygındır. Tempo genellikle dakikada 140 ila 150 vuruş arasında değişir.

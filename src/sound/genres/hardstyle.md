  Hardstyle is a form of hard dance that emerged from hard trance with strong hardcore influences,

  Hard, four-on-the-floor kick drums, fast tempo around 150 BPM, reverse bass

  Hardstyle is characterized by its persistent, compressed, repetitive, distorted kick drums, and its use of the reverse bass, and harsh, distorted synths. Melodies are often uplifting and emotional, contrasting with the aggressive beats. Breakdown sections are common, providing contrast to the intense drops. The tempo generally ranges from 140 to 150 beats per minute.

description_en: |-
  Power electronics is a term denoting a style of noise music that focuses on heavy use of loud high-end and/or low-end frequencies. Typically it consists of static, screeching waves of feedback, analogue synthesizers making sub-bass pulses or high frequency squealing sounds; with (sometimes) screamed and distorted vocals. Power electronics is generally atonal, like most noise music; it also features a lack of conventional melodies or rhythms.

  Like the name suggests, power electronics heavily uses synthesizers and other such electronic equipment to create loud screeching sounds alongside the occasional usage of power tools and metal scraps like sheets and bars. Power electronics musicians use these instruments to create a layered sound heavily inspired by the industrial movement.

  Often, lyrics in songs with vocals in this style discuss subject matter considered taboo to the general public, like rape and violence, often addressing them in a blunt and forceful manner, with the intended goal of shocking those who listen to it.

  A similarly brooding subgenre, death industrial, brought a substantially large dark ambient influence into the traditional formula of power electronics.

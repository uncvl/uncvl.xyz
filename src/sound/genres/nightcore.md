  Nightcore is genre of electronic music that is based on speeding up pre-existing songs, increasing the BPM to be usually near 200.

  The genre is characterized by high-pitched vocals and a generally cutesy tone. Releases typically take from eurodance and vocal vrance, but more recent releases have taken a broader approach to production, often remixing Alternative Rock, Contemporary R&B, and Indie Folk.

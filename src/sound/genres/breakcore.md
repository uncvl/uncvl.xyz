description_en: |-
  Breakcore is characterized by very complex and intricate breakbeats and a wide palette of sampling sources played at high tempos. Typically fast (180BPM+), loud and distorted breakbeat hardcore.

  Song structure is often non-linear. The breakbeats don't stay the same throughout the song, like in drum and bass, but rather different breaks are chopped, changed and mashed together. Melodically, there is nothing that defines breakcore. Classic rave sounds such as acid bass lines are common, but breakcore is mostly known for sampling sounds from all over the musical spectrum to accommodate the frantic and fast-paced nature of the rhythm section.

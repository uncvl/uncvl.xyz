description_en: |-
  Hyperpop is a loosely-defined music movement and microgenre which is characterized by a maximalist or exaggerated take on popular music. Artists within the genre typically integrate pop and avant-garde sensibilities while drawing on themes commonly found in electronic, hip hop, and dance music.

  Hyperpop sticks to standard verse-chorus pop songwriting but emphasizes an eclectic, unconventional, off-kilter, and sometimes abrasive sound design unlike mainstream pop, such as unconventional rhythms, metallic and heavily compressed percussion, distorted synth leads, chaotic, dense production, and futuristic, layered synth work.

  Hyperpop reflects an exaggerated, eclectic, and self-referential approach to pop music and typically employs elements such as brash synth melodies, auto-tuned vocals, glitch-inspired percussion and excessive compression and distortion, as well as surrealist or nostalgic references to 2000s Internet culture with a distinctive late-capitalism-dystopia vibe.

  Digicore and glitchcore are contemporaneous movements that are sometimes conflated with hyperpop due to its overlapping artists.

  The influence of cloud rap, emo and lo-fi trap, trance music, dubstep, and chiptune are evident in hyperpop, as well as more surreal and haphazard qualities that are pulled heavily from hip hop.

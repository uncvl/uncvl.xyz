description_en: |-
  Sludge metal is a subgenre of metal that combines the slow, dark instrumentals and atmosphere of doom metal with the aggressive, harsh screams and shouts with influences from both punk music and metal music. The term "sludge" is a metaphor referring to the slow and dingy atmosphere this blend of metal and punk invokes.

  It is typically harsh and abrasive, often featuring angry, pained vocals vocals, heavily distorted instruments, guitar feedback, groovy riffs and sharply contrasting tempos. Lyrical themes borrow both from the angry social and political commentary of punk and from the sad and melancholic themes of doom metal. This amalgamates into music that can best be described as frustrated and embittered, often either at society or at oneself.

  Sludge is also considered to be a major influence on modern powerviolence.

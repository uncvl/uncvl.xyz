description_en: |-
  Sextrance is a microgenre of hard trance music that draws influence from multiple other musical genres, including hexD, nightcore, and freeform hardcore.. 

  It's a bitcrushed, distorted style of trance with a progressive and immersive soundscape that incorporates breakcore and hardcore elements. Bitcrushed, compressed, abrasive synths, and highly processed, distorted sounds used to build up an energetic and emotionally charged atmosphere that precedes a bass drop. Tempo is fast, usually around 160-200 BPM.

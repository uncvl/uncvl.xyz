description_en: |-
  Hardvapour is defined by the fusion of elements of vaporwave music with other elements of harder and faster genres such as hardcore, speedcore, noise, industrial and drum'n bass. Common elements include thick synthesizer sounds, programmed drums, fast tempos. Hardvapour moved away from the vaporwave's more ambient atmospheres in favor of a hardcore and punk-influenced, darker, more aggressive and rhythmic sound.

  The scene would primarily distinguish itself from other vaporwave offsprings by largely being a reaction against its messages and aesthetics. They departed from the calm, muzak-sampling capitalist utopia concept and 1980s pop cliches in favor of cultural artifacts associated with socialism and eastern Europe. Artists will often also use Cyrillic characters in their album and song titles to contrast with the use of Japanese script commonly used in vaporwave.

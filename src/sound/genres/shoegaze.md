description_en: |-
  Shoegaze is a subgenre of indie and alternative rock characterized by its ethereal mixture of obscured vocals, overwhelming volume, guitar distortion and effects such as feedback, reverb and delay.

  Shoegaze combines swirling vocals with layers of distorted, bent, or flanged guitars, creating a wash of sound where no instrument is distinguishable from another. The guitars often reach overwhelming volumes, making the lyrics unintelligible and mainly applying vocals as another melodic layer.

  It often overlaps with the related genre of dream pop, and the two genre terms have at times been used interchangeably.

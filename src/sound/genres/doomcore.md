description_en: |-
  Doomcore is the slower, and darker style of the hardcore techno.

  The speed of doomcore tracks ranges between 130 and 160 BPM. The trademark TR-909 kickdrum is used differently than in other hardcore genres: it usually employs less distortion, and less decay. However, a reverb effect is added on it sometimes. Offbeat cymbals are usual. The atmosphere of the tracks is often similar to industrial music, doom metal and dark ambient. Producers reach this impression by using spooky ambient pads, different scary or screaming samples, and industrial noises. Repetition of the main melody is a common practice.

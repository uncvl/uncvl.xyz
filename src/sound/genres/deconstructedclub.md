description_en: |-
  Deconstructed club (aka post-club, experimental club, club-not-club, avant-club) has an abrasive or dystopian tone and identified by aggressive, frantic sound design featuring metallic, explosive percussion or samples of glass smashing, gunshots, etc. Deconstructed club aims for an excessive, apocalyptic-sounding soundscape, with constant rhythmic switch-ups and atonality.

  The genre is characterized by its disruptive elements and a wide dynamic tempo range, often utilizing jersey club kick-patterns, grime claps, and jittery footwork production to create a sensation of frenetic high-BPM tracks. In addition, tracks delve into experimental soundscapes and alternating atmospheric breathers.

  Deconstructed club also uses elements of regional sounds as a reaction to underrepresentation of non-English-speaking cultures. It often addresses themes such as race relations, feminism, queer politics and colonialism, or concepts such as futurism and technology.

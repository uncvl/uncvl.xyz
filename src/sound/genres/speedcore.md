description_en: |-
  Speedcore is a form of hardcore that is characterized by a high tempo and aggressive themes. This music rarely drops below 300 bpm. The genre is further divided into splittercore when the range goes from 600 to 1000 BPM, and extratone, which begins at 1000 and has no set limit. The genre has notable crossovers with breakcore.

  Speedcore can often be distinguished from other forms of hardcore by an aggressive and overridden electronic percussion track that is often punctuated with a hyperactive snare or tom-tom fills. Speedcore DJs often use violent, vulgar, and offensive themes in their music to push the boundaries of the genre.

description_en: |-
  Drift phonk is a form of hip hop rooted electronic music characterized by the use of high tempo, high bass, heavy use of 808 cowbells and distorted sounds, making the lyrics of the samples often unrecognizable.

  It is characterized by busy, noisy production inspired by lo-fi Memphis rap beats. Heavy compression, distorted percussion, and rhythms inspired by trap are sometimes used as well. The genre features samples of oldschool Memphis rap and other rap tracks, which are usually heavily edited, looped, and buried in the mix.

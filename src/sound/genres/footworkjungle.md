description_en: |-
  Combining Footwork's archetypal percussion, skittering drum fills, handclaps, and syncopated rhythms, with Drum and Bass drum programming and breakbeats, merging the two into unconventional, frantic drumming and dense, off-kilter rhythms.

  It marries chopped Amen/Think breaks, sub-heavy 808 bass, and halftime/duple-feel syncopation with footwork-style vocal chops, call‑and‑response samples, and spacious, rave‑born atmospherics. The result is music that can flex between heads‑down listening and high‑intensity dance movement, retaining jungle’s rolling momentum while adopting footwork’s off‑grid micro‑rhythm and negative space. Like most footwork and like various forms of DnB, footwork jungle is typically produced and mixed at 160 BPM.

  Though the genre's name may hint at a close connection with Jungle, not all producers include jungle elements, though it is a common influence. Other DnB genres such as Atmospheric Drum and Bass, Drumfunk or even Jump-Up may be present as well.

description_en: |-
  Microfunk is a minimalist style of drum and bass. It favors space, subtlety, and micro‑edited textures over big drops and maximal energy. It is characterized by a minimal, glitchy, and "bleep"-y sound inspired by IDM.

  Rhythms are often half‑time or sparsely syncopated, sub‑bass stays pure and sine‑leaning, and melodies are concise and emotive. It iIncorporates quiet percussion, deep sub-bass, and subdued melodies, often picks influences from other drum and bass styles like liquid drum and bass, atmospheric drum and bass, and neurofunk.

description_en: |-
  Industrial hardcore is a form of hardcore music, mainly characterized by its raw form of production, usually containing tuned or clipped bassdrum patterns with mechanical, sometimes distorted sound design, in addition to soundscapes related to dark ambient, industrial and noise.

  Industrial hardcore largely emerged from developments between techno, industrial and related electronic styles of music. The genre went to establish itself by a widespread pool of gabber, speedcore and terrorcore producers. Later the genre saw closer cross-pollination with the slower forms of doomcore and hardstyle. It has also been infrequently experimental in nature. Several DJ units and labels were highly inspired by the underground styles of IDM, glitch, power noise and original industrial music. These affiliated artists became characterized by their abstract sound design and unorthodox rhythmic and compositional structures that would heavily influence future developments in flashcore and splittercore.

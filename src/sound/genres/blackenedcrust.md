description_en: |-
  Blackened crust borrows heavily from the sound of second wave black metal while retaining the characteristic lyricism, aesthetics, and modern sound of crust punk.

  The black metal influence comes from the lo-fi end of the spectrum, manifesting as cavernous production quality, screeched or screamed vocals, and periodic use of blast beats and tremolo guitar. Lyrics often deal with crust punk’s nihilistic and political themes, occult and mystical black metal motifs, and general misanthropy shared by both.

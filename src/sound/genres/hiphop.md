description_en: |-
  Hip hop is a form of music emphasizing rhythmic beat patterns and spoken delivery rather than harmonic melodies and sung vocals.

  It consists of stylized rhythmic music (usually built around drum beats) that commonly accompanies rapping, a rhythmic and rhyming speech that is chanted. Other elements include sampling beats or bass lines from records (or synthesized beats and sounds), and rhythmic beatboxing. The genre may also incorporate other elements of hip hop culture, including DJing, turntablism, scratching and instrumental tracks.

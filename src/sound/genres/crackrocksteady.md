  Crack rock steady is a niche subgenre of skacore that utilizes elements of crust punk and sometimes extreme metal.

  The styles of riffs range from clean skanks to heavier, thrash metal-derived riffs. The pace of the style varies, ranging from moderate tempos to dizzyingly fast speeds. A rough and aggressive sound is a consistent characteristic of the genre, contrasting with the comparatively playful image of ska punk.

  Akin to hardcore punk, the vocals tend to be brash, however, are often used in melodic ways. It lyrically and aesthetically focuses on similar themes to crust punk. Lyrics often focus on themes such as anti-capitalism, anarchism, politics, social issues, religion and drug-use.

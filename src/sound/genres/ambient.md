description_en: |-
  Ambient music is a genre of music that emphasizes tone and atmosphere over traditional musical structure or rhythm. It may lack net composition, beat, or structured melody. It uses textural layers of sound that encourage a sense of calm or contemplation.

  As a genre, ambient focuses on creating a mood or atmosphere through synthesizers and timbral qualities. It often lacks the presence of any net composition, beat, or structured melody. Since it is a relatively ambiguous term, ambient has no distinct characteristics, and its style can vary a lot.

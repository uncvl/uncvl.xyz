description_en: |-
  Skullstep is a style of darkstep. It is characterized by repetitious, aggressive, off-beat rhythms often programmed using distorted, harsh, and noisy percussion that usually resembles clangy "pots and pans", machine gun-like snares, which are blended with the already dark and sinister-sounding atmospheres of darkstep. Certain producers also implement influences from various styles of hardcore, post-industrial, and even noise music.

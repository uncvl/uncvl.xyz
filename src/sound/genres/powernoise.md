description_en: |-
  Power noise (or rhythmic noise) is a subgenre of industrial music that combines looped noise samples with various types of electronic-derived rhythms. In particular, there are stylistic parallels to electro-industrial, industrial techno, hardcore, industrial hardcore and breakcore such that, unlike in regular noise compositions, the sounds themselves are not as pure a form of noise as the name suggests.

  Traditionally, power noise is instrumental and based upon heavily distorted drum machines, abrasive synthesizers and martial but danceable 4/4 beats. Occasionally, melodic components and abstract soundscapes are added secondarily to the rhythmic structure.

description_en: |-
  Noise pop is a subgenre of alternative and indie rock that is defined by the combination of conventional pop songwriting with experimental sounds of dissonant noise or feedback, distorted guitars and drones. The style often has a hazy, narcotic feel, as melodies drift through the swirling guitar textures. But it can also be bright and lively, or angular and challenging.

  Noise pop's combination of sunny, simple melodies with waves of distortion sets it apart from the rawer, less melodic, often haphazard sounds of noise rock. The vocals in noise pop also tend to be hazier, dreamier and altogether more melodic and harmonic than in noise rock.

  The sound would be one influence on the rise of shoegaze a few years later.

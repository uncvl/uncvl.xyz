description_en: |-
  Freeform hardcore, also originally known as trancecore, is a substyle of hardcore music which heavily draws elements from trance music.

  In contrast to happy hardcore, freeform productions tend to be more instrumental-oriented while incorporating straight 4/4 beat patterns, faster tempos around 150-200 BPM, acid-style basslines, and a more intense and synthesized production in general. Freeform tends to be darker, more psychedelic and through‑composed, borrowing heavily from trance, hard trance and psytrance while keeping the relentless energy of hardcore techno for the dancefloor. Hallmarks include epic, evolving synth leads (often supersaws), minor‑key and modal harmonies, acid lines, and dramatic breakdowns that resolve into driving drops.

Freeform, dans pisti için hardcore techno’nun durmak bilmeyen enerjisini korurken, trance, hard trance ve psytrance’ten yoğun bir şekilde esinlenerek daha karanlık, daha psikedelik ve baştan sona bestelenmiş bir yapıya sahiptir. Bu tarzın ayırt edici özellikleri arasında epik, değişken synth lead’leri (genellikle supersaw’lar), minör tonlu ve modal armoniler, acid hatları ve sürükleyici drop’lara dönüşen dramatik breakdown’lar yer alır.

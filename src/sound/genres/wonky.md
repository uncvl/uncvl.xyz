description_en: |-
  Wonky (also known as street bass, purple sound or aquacrunk) is the name given to a fusion of dubstep and hip hop (particularly glitch hop). It is defined by its off-kilter and unstable, wonky mid-range, with unquantised and offbeat hip hop rhythms at dubstep tempos.

  Wonky typically features garish synthesizer tones, melodies, and effects. The unstable sound of wonky is often achieved by producing unquantised beats, abandoning the metronomic precision of much electronic music. Artists also use heavy sub-bass.

  Wonky is known for its eclectic, colorful blend of genres including hip hop, electro-funk, chiptune, jazz fusion, glitch, and crunk.

description_en: |-
  Cloud rap is a form of hip hop characterized by a distinct form of relaxed, ethereal, dream-like, and reverb-heavy production. Cloud rap commonly involves heavier, more detailed electronic production in comparison to many earlier hip hop production styles. It has several sonic characteristics of lo-fi in its hazy, dreamlike and relaxed sound.

  One of the early influence on cloud rap was the then-developing vaporwave, and its distinct DIY approach and aesthetics, along with its hyper-real and retro-futuristic atmosphere were incredibly influential to cloud rap, and to internet-based music in general. Cloud rap is rhythmically similar to lo-fi and chill-wave but distinguishes itself with distorted, psychedelic samples and the inclusion of rap. Trap music's massive spark in size and influence was also beginning to show its effects and many cloud rap producers and rappers implemented its rhythms and typical flows. Producers of the genre also combined cloud rap's atmospheric and melancholic beats with sampling from emo genres. Additionally it is influenced by wide variety of genres including hip hop, drum and bass, grime, trip hop, indie, rock, and pop music genres.

  Though early cloud rap waves were commonly characterized by more esoteric lyricism than mainstream hip hop, such as pop culture references, over-the-top humor, and surreal or fantasy-themed lyrics, the genre always featured more typical hip hop themes like sex, drug use, alienation. That being said, many abstract hip hop artists have commonly implemented influences of the genre, whose more atmospheric, outlandish soundscapes are used as a palette for their abstract, poetic lyricism. Cloud rap also often utilizes looped samples from female singers, and often from those whose voices have an ethereal quality.

  Wave developed as a blend of earlier cloud rap production with prominent influences of EDM genres. Plugg and rare phonk both derived from trap, were heavily inspired by cloud rap's atmospheric, lush and laid-back sound.

  The term 'cloud rap' is related to its internet birth, its ethereal style and its ambiguity as a genre without clearly defined borders. Often, cloud rap is released independently of record labels. Sites like Bandcamp and SoundCloud are commonly used platforms for cloud rap artists.

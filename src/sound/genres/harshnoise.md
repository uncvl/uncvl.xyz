description_en: |-
  Harsh noise is a term applied to noise music that seeks to take the genre to its natural limits, resulting in hugely aggressive walls of sound that assault the listener. It is characterized by a distinct lack of traditional musicality and form, instead relying on sounds generated from signal processing or other electronic means to create atmosphere.

  Unlike standard noise, harsh noise tends to be more dissonant and unpleasant to listen to, due to the wall of loudness and general atonality. Vocals of any sort are usually absent, as are any discernible forms of instrumentation.

  Like its cousin drone, songs can drag on for tens of minutes, if not over an hour or more.

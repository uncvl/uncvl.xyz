description_en: |-
  Trap is a subgenre of hip hop music characterized by complex and fast hi-hat patterns, tuned heavy kick drums with a long decay. It utilizes very few instruments and focuses almost exclusively on snare drums and double or triple timed hi-hats. Tempo is usually around 140 BPM.

  Trap music employs multilayered thin or thick textured monophonic drones with sometimes a melodic accompaniment expressed with synthesizers; crisp, grimy, and rhythmic snares, deep 808 kick drums, double-time, triple-time, and similarly divided hi-hats, and a cinematic and symphonic use of synthesized string, brass, woodwind, and keyboard instruments to create an energetic, hard-hitting, deep, and variant atmosphere.

  Trap began to diversify in underground scenes, spawning a variety of different offshoots of the sound. Several of these would arise from SoundCloud scenes, like trap metal, a genre characterized by distorted and noisy production and sometimes screamed/shouted vocals; emo rap, a sound derived from trap and cloud rap that combined atmospheric beats with emo influences in both sampling and vocals; plugg, a more minimal and laid-back style; tread, a distinctive style of high-speed and rhythmically busy trap production; and rare phonk, an instrumental hip hop focused form of trap that incorporates memphis rap elements. Trap influence on contemporary electronic music would continue, as with wave, which blended cloud rap influences with trap rhythms, elements of witch house and forms of EDM.

description_en: |-
  Dub is an electronic musical style that grew out of reggae. Dub was a basis for the genres of jungle and drum and bass, with its orientation around bass and utilization of audio effects.

  Generally, dub consists of remixes of existing recordings created by significantly manipulating the original, usually through the removal of vocal parts, the application of studio effects such as echo and reverb, emphasis of the rhythm section, and the occasional dubbing of vocal or instrumental snippets from the original version or other works.

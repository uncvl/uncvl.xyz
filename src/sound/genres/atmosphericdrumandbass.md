description_en: |-
  Atmospheric drum and bass is a style of drum and bass that prioritizes mood, texture, and space over aggression. It blends rapid breakbeats and deep sub‑bass with lush pads, airy reverbs, and melodic motifs.

  Typically sitting around 160–174 BPM, atmospheric drum and bass puts more of a focus on percussive elements, such as light tambourines and foley supporting the melodic elements, accompanying ambience in the tracks. Chord extensions (7ths/9ths/11ths), evolving drones, and field recordings create a cinematic, introspective feel that contrasts with the music’s propulsive rhythm section. An ethereal soundscape is accomplished through the liberal use of reverb, delay, and filtering, as well as deep, filtered pads / plucks and focus on an ample stereoaural field. A deep sub or reese bass oftentimes accompanies the other ambient elements.

description_en: |-
  Wave emerged as a style of electronic music heavily influenced by 2010s hip hop production. It is characterized by dark, icy, nocturnal and melancholic beats, melodic and heavy bass, atmospheric melodies and harmonies, modern trap drums, chopped vocal samples and arpeggiators.

  Wave blends cloud rap's hazy and dreamy atmospheres with darker and more sinister ambiance, sometimes resembling witch house and with rhythms borrowed from trap and influenced to varying degrees by dubstep, grime, future garage and UK bass. Wave is typically bass heavy.

  The percussion features trap-style drums with fast hi-hats, with other elements like snare and pan-hits further processed using reverb. Wave emphasizes melodic and harmonic aspects using tools like arpeggiation and atmospheric pads. Some producers incorporate prominent, melodic synth leads (often supersaws or sawtooth waves) that are sometimes made loud and distorted, in addition to reverberated synth pads. Vocals used are generally chopped samples, for which reverb and delay effects are applied to decrease and increase the pitch. Heavily processed vocal samples and dense sci-fi soundscapes are also common.

  Wave attempts to convey emotions such as melancholy, and qualities such as dreaminess, femininity, and otherworldliness.

  Wave incorporates elements from many other genres such as hip hop, dubstep, UK garage, cloud rap, drill, vaporwave, chiptune, ambient, jungle, house, techno and sound design.

description_en: |-
  Darkstep is a subgenre of drum and bass that fuses elements of darkcore with uptempo breakbeats and ambient noises. Darkstep is characterised by fast drums and a general dark mood, drawing influences from dark ambient, industrial and hardcore music.

  Darkstep music is typically composed in a chromatic scale. The amen break, the firefight break, and other breakbeat samples feature prominently.

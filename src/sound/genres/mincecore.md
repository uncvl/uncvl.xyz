description_en: |-
  Mincecore is a raw and simplistic form of grindcore which is based in hardcore punk and takes comparatively little influence from metal.

  The guitar riffs are simple and heavily distorted and the drumming is characterized by alternation between blast beats and a groovy, mid-tempo kick-snare-kick-snare pattern. Lyrical themes are socio-political. They include topics such as anarchism, veganism, antifascism and offer resistance against sexism, homophobia and racism.

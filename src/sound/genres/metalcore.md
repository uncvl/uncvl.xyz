description_en: |-
  Metalcore (also known as metallic hardcore) is a fusion music genre that combines elements of extreme metal and hardcore punk. What differentiates metalcore from other combinations of metal and hardcore punk, such as crust punk and grindcore, is the emphasis on breakdowns, slow, intense passages conducive to moshing and greater use of dissonance.

  The instrumentation of metalcore includes heavy guitar riffs often utilizing percussive pedal tones, stop-start rhythm guitar, double bass drumming, and breakdowns. Drop guitar tunings are often used. Drummers typically use a lot of double bass technique and general drumming styles across the board. Blast beats are also heard at times. Vocalists in the genre typically use thrash or scream vocals. Death growls and gang vocals are common.

description_en: |-
  Screamo (also referred to as skramz) is a style of hardcore punk influenced emo with screaming. It merges the emotional lyrics and melodicism of emo with the intensity, aggression and speed of hardcore punk. Lyrical themes usually include emotional pain, death, romance, and human rights.

  Screamo uses typical rock instrumentation, but is notable for its brief compositions, chaotic sounds and harmonized guitars. Emotional singing and harsh screaming are common vocals in screamo. Screamo lyrics often feature topics such as emotional pain, breakups, romantic interest, politics, and human rights. These lyrics are usually introspective and have a sense of acceptance of differences, respect for otherness.

  Screamo tends to be complex in structure, featuring frequent shifts in tempo and rhythm. Many screamo bands also incorporate ballads.

  Many bands have been introducing new stylistic elements from the genres like mathcore, sludge metal and post rock. Emoviolence, which took influence from powerviolence is a subgenre of screamo.

description_en: |-
  Crossbreed is also known as brokencore, core & bass, drum & core, hardcore drum & bass. Developing as a mixture of darkstep with hardcore, crossbreed was born out of the equal influence of both genres. It is characterized by powerful, harsh 4/4 rhythms influenced by gabber's distorted beats, which are mixed with the high-speed, amen-derived breaks, drum and bass drum programming, and gloomy, claustrophobic, and horror-related textures and atmospheres of darkstep. Certain producers have additionally introduced influences of breakcore and post-industrial music, particularly industrial hardcore.

description_en: |-
  Breakstep is a subgenre of UK garage, differentiated from other subgenres by its use of hard-hitting breakbeat rhythms, rather than shuffling 2-Step or 4x4 rhythms, as well as its focus on heavy, rolling basslines, taking influence from genres such as nu skool breaks and techstep. Breakstep tracks also tend to lack the contemporary R&B vocals found in UK garage.

description_en: Breakstep is a subgenre of UK garage, differentiated from other subgenres by its use of hard-hitting breakbeat rhythms, rather than shuffling 2-Step or 4x4 rhythms, as well as its focus on heavy, rolling basslines, taking influence from genres such as nu skool breaks and techstep. Breakstep tracks also tend to lack the contemporary R&B vocals found in UK garage.
description_tr: Breakstep, UK garage’ın bir alt türüdür; diğer alt türlerden, shuffling 2-Step veya 4x4 ritimleri yerine sert breakbeat ritimlerinin kullanılmasıyla ve nu skool breaks ile techstep gibi türlerden esinlenerek yoğun, akıcı bassline'lara ağırlık vermesiyle ayrılır. Breakstep parçalarında genellikle UK garage’da rastlanan modern R&B vokalleri bulunmaz.

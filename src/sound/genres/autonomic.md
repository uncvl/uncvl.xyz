description_en: |-
  Autonomic also known as halfstep is a style of drum and bass with a unique and evocative style, characterised by its deep, atmospheric, and minimalist approach.

  Autonomic is characterised by its lush and immersive atmospheres, intricate soundscapes, pad textures, and carefully crafted reverb to create a sense of depth. It lacks the dense and heavy amen breaks and/or busy percussion of most drum and bass styles, instead focusing on simpler, metallic drums and repetitive rhythms, often played at half-times, slower tempos. Producers incorporate unconventional time signatures, intricate drum patterns, and off-kilter rhythms. The beats are sparse and syncopated, allowing the listener to focus on the interplay between the elements. Vocal samples, when used, are often manipulated and integrated into the music as textural elements rather than traditional hooks.

  It is influenced by dubstep and future garage and incorporates elements from the futuristic, dark sound of techstep.

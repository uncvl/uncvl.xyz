description_en: |-
  Eccojams is an early subgenre of vaporwave. It started out as a simple exercise looping up a slowed-down segment of a certain song while adding vibrating echoes and established the template that most vaporwave musicians used to transform a whole world of forgotten pop culture into a legitimate mode of expression. It is more of a method than a subgenre.

  Eccojams are usually made from a single short loop of the source material. Featuring samples from the 70s, 80s and 90s, the sub-genre is distinct for its use of smothering effects like reverb and echo. Changing the tempo, using distortions and dissections are common practices that eccojams producers apply.

  Subgenre offered methods to create new music using old songs and its sonic manipulations allowed producers to reveal old tracks in a new light. If there’s one vaporwave subgenre that’s more or less identical to vaporwave itself, it’s eccojams.

  Jumpstyle is a style of Hard Dance that is derived from genres like hardstyle, gabber, hardcore techno, hard house, hard dance, and mákina.

  jumpstyle is characterized by a heavy, distorted sound with hard 909 kick drum, a four-on-the-floor beat, sharp basslines, square-wave synths, and looping melodies. It sits between the intensity of gabber and the drive of hardstyle, usually around 140–150 BPM. Melodies in Jumpstyle often feature simple, repetitive patterns, frequently utilizing synthesizers and often incorporating elements of trance or hardstyle.

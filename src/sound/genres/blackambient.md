  Black ambient is a subgenre of dark ambient music, which incorporates in its sound some elements usually associated with the black metal scene. The slow and dark atmosphere typical of dark ambient is taken to extreme levels of dirtiness and is often complemented by harsh shrieks and haunting whispers, resulting in a very dark, sinister, and hypnotic sound.

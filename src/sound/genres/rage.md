description_en: |-
  Rage (also known as rage beats or hypertrap) is a subgenre of trap. It is characterized by dense, repetitive synth work inspired by electronic dance music, mainly future bass, mostly basic “buzzing” sounding waveforms over conventional trap drum programming, typically performed in an energetic rap style.

  Song structures are unorthodox and repetitive. The departure from conventional hip hop song structure contributed to rage's distinct 'punk ethos'.

  Rage has been heavily influenced by subgenres like plugg, digicore and cloud rap.

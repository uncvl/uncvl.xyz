description_en: |-
  Drill is a form of hip hop, broadly characterized by complex, energetic rhythms, coupled with street-conscious and often violent rapping.

  Drill is rooted in trap, first appearing as Chicago drill. UK drill appeared in the UK with heavy grime influences. Sample drill, a sample-focused style of production, appeared in New York. Jersey drill similarly mixes New York drill production styles with Jersey club influences.

  Though it bears many similarities to trap music the speed of a drill beat is generally slower with a moderate tempo having about 60 to 70 beats per minute.

  Drill rappers' lack of concern with metaphor or wordplay. Drill lyrics typically reflect life on the streets, and tend to be gritty, violent, realistic and nihilistic. Drill rappers use a grim, deadpan delivery often filtered through auto-tune. While early Chicago drill rappers tended to use a distinct monotonous, drawling flow, drill developed more rapid, aggressive styles of delivery that became more widespread with the popularity of UK drill.

  Drill would also influence forms of electronic music, with certain wave artists for example incorporating drill-derived rhythms.

description_en: |-
  Atmospheric black metal (also known as ambient black metal) is a subgenre of black metal known for its significant influence from ambient music. Atmospheric black metal is characterized by its focus on creating dark or happy atmospheres, dreamy or ethereal textures, long songs, and lyrics generally about nature, paganism, folklore, mythology, and personal introspection.

  The focus of this style is the atmospheric aspect of black metal which is expanded upon by slowly-evolving riffs and repetitive melodies. It would achieve its bleak, icy atmosphere by using an incredibly lo-fi recording, high treble production, and minimalistic arrangements. Usually, the songs are slow to mid-paced and feature commonly synthesized ambient textures.

  Later the genre expanded its sound into different directions. Some bands used keyboards, synthesizers, and programmed drums as primary instruments, with the guitar becoming a secondary layer to their sound, creating symphonic soundscapes. Some bands doubled down the lo-fi aesthetic and create an extremely rawer style with denser atmospheres, where instruments are almost indistinguishable from one another; colloquially dubbed "no-fi." In opposition to this trend, through rich usage of guitar effects and cleaner production a more ethereal, lush style of atmo-black was created and it later become known as blackgaze. Later on some elements of post-rock, doom metal or industrial metal were also combined with atmospheric black metal.

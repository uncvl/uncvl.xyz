description_en: |-
  **Garage**, used here as a metagenre term, refers to:

  * deep, rolling, syncopated basslines, especially sub-bass lines
  * 4/4 house kick patterns or more irregular 2-step rhythms
  * percussive, shuffled rhythms with syncopated hi-hats, cymbals, and snares
  * heavy bass kick and a strong snare with open and closed hi-hats
  * chopped up and time-stretched or pitch-shifted vocal samples underlying the rhythmic structure

  Garage used here refers to the UK Garage and derivatives, not Garage Rock.

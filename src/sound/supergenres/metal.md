  **Metal**, used here as a metagenre term, refers to:

  * emphasis on loud distorted guitars (high volumes and heavy fuzz)
  * powerful rhythms, dense bass-and-drum sound
  * prominent role of the bass providing the low-end sound which makes the sound heavy
  * loud, constant beat (double-kicks, blast beats)
  * vigorous (shouted, growled, shrieked) vocals and heavy lyrical content (aggression, dismay, rebellion against society)

description_en: |-
  **Ska**, used here as a metagenre term, refers to:

  * a guitar-strumming technique called skank (The skank involves adding a guitar chop on the bass line’s offbeat)
  * a walking bassline, which means the bass notes constantly move up and down the scale
  * instrumental riffs played by brass instruments (saxophone, trumpet, trombone)
  * 4/4 time signature
  * frequent jumpy rhythms

  Drummers tend to emphasize the backbeat (beats two and four), and guitarists often make percussive stabs on beats two, three, and four. The bass guitar tends to be active, playing a mix of quarter notes and eighth notes.

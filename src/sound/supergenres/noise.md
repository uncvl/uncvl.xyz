  **Noise**, used here as a metagenre term, refers to:

  * expressive use of acoustically or electronically generated noise
  * use of randomly produced electronic signals such as distortion, feedback, static, hiss and hum
  * emphasis on high volume levels
  * association with improvisation, extended technique, cacophony and indeterminacy
  * lack of conventional use of melody, harmony, rhythm or pulse

text_en: |-
  Concept refers to the fictional aspects of the project.

  There is a concept by which we develop themes. These themes serve as an intellectual and creative feeding base for the project.

  Themes specify frames by making references to certain incidents, locations, time periods, topics, etc. They aim to encourage the participant to think about the given references and come up with a creative idea within the specified frame.

  The concept determines the overall direction, and themes determine the areas of research.

  - title_en: Existence / Extinction
    title_tr: ""
    text_en: |-
      The main concept is built on two pairs of notions: **existence – extinction** and **civilization – uncivilization**.

      Together they point to certain moments in time and place. When and where civilization came into existence is our starting point: putting ourselves at that moment, we try to understand how and why it happened. From there, we speculate about when and how civilization will cease to exist.

      Between the two ends lie the mass extinctions, the extinction events that shaped the origination and collapse of civilizations. We question the roles of nature and humans in these crucial turning points.

      > … from nothing to being there is no logical bridge.
      >
      > — William James, “On Some Hegelisms”, 1882

      Researchers and producers work on it together. Research feeds the world, and the world inspires and catalyzes production. The project is also an experiment in itself: can we find methods to build a system by which we can collaboratively develop a fictional world?

text_en: |-
  > When we consider Uncivilization as an organization, it should be viewed as the structure that constitutes and operates it. One of Uncivilization’s primary goals is to develop organizational practices. To this end, we aim to develop methods for collective decision-making and collaborative production, and to use communication channels and tools of production effectively and consciously.

  ‘Organizational entity’ refers to the network of people involved in the activities of Uncivilization.

  These people preferably come together in real life at the events of Uncivilization and get to know each other while working together. Instead of appointment or application basis, they become a part of the organizational entity organically during the processes. If they feel fit and gain acceptence they become a part of it. This is why action aspect of the project is crucial and necessary for the formation and expansion of the organizational entity.

  Following are some terms we use for the organization. These are used to distribute roles, responsibilities, and initiative as well as to set the hierarchy of decision-making.

  - Core
  - Collective
  - Circle
  - Administrators
  - Work Groups
    - Organizational Teams
    - Research Groups
    - Creative Crews
    - Tech Team
  - Skill Pools

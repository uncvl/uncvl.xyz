  Da Frogg, one of the pioneers of Turkish bass music culture, and GKC, known for her high-energy Old Skool House sets, will come together on stage. The duo, who exhibited a brand new performance with both back to back and "tandem" styles by using 4 decks in 2 different setups, is coming to Izmir to play their long sets combining Dub, Rap, Breakbeat, Garage, Jungle and many other genres. The event, which will take place on Saturday, November 1, at Audiodar, a performance space in Darağaç, the art-filled neighborhood of Izmir, will start at 17:00 and end at 21:30.

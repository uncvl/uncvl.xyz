text_en: |
  KRAYAT, a migrant DIY punk/hardcore band, is coming from Berlin to İstanbul and İzmir. They're going to be on stage at Kadıköy Sahne and Dinozor on 26-27 September, Thursday and Friday). Krayat will share the stage with local punk bands GOBLIN DAYCARE and POTLATCH in İstanbul and RUFF CRUX, MOKO and HUSH in İzmir.

  We wish that two punk concerts, organized by Uncivilization, would provide a nice opportunity for the bands, crews, friends and individuals of diverse geography to come together, interact, stick to the punk-ethos and feel comfort in our distinctive underground culture.

  Our distinctive culture demands a massive social and institutional transformation in order to change the way we are governed.

  Authority needs to be smashed.
  Hierarchy needs to be flattened.

  Dropping the capitalist apparatus,
  DIY and mutual aid are the way to go.
  Temporary autonomy in a permanent manner.
  Participatory, collective community in every part of social life.
  Thinking and acting combined in a common effort for an individual cause: Freedom.

  KRAYAT is formed in 2019 and they're playing around Europe since 2020. On August 2024 their new 7inch “For breakfast paranoia, for dinner what?” is out by Autsajder Produkcija from Croatia and Χάος και Αντικομφορμισμός from Greece.

  Check them out, They're in Bandcamp.

text_en: |-
  Is it only music, art, and entertainment that brings us together? Or is it something deeper, a consensus on our worldview?

  What unites us is not instructions or guidelines from a central point, but the attitudes and choices of individuals.

  We have named our event DECENTRALIZED CONSENSUS, to signify that.

  We will come together for music, visuals, performance, zines, and more.

  We'll be setting up a table for visuals. Our devices will be connected, and projection equipment will scatter bursts of photons around. Aziz Deliorman, Algorizma crew, and Buffer Overflow will be in charge of this throughout the event.

  The music will never stop throughout the event. We'll start with the chirping of Pocket Operators and continue with punk, breakcore, hardstyle, and hardcore.

  Also, at some point during the event, a performance prepared by Gowno will erupt. Let's see what this clown, who enjoys disturbing our peace with his grotesque works, will do this time.

  Never Grow Up resurrected the human figure in the Uncivilization emblem using plastic bottles. Hopefully, with the support of Abyssal Area we plan to complete our composition in the emblem by adding new elements 🌠.

  And we've been gathering zines from zine makers. Nep will distribute them at our event along with the recycled pins he produced.

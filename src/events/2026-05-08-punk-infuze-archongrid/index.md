text_en: |-
  Welcome to "infuse_archonGrid"! We are meeting at nomads36 (ex-f.loki) on Friday, May 8 at 21.00 to surrender our minds and souls to the grid with live music and gameplay!

  In our event, we point out the feeling of the moment when we go to our desks, take a deep breath and press the start button after a busy work, school and general hustle and bustle.

  In this journey, FEDERICO FELLINI will give us the start with his experimental no-wave tones. After we are roasted by noise, HYSTERIA will take over the stage. After pumping euphoria with shoegaze and noise rock with a feeling of nostalgia to our bones, the sound will be handed to URBAN DIVINE, the last stop in the concert phase. The band, which interprets sludge and stoner within the framework of progressive hardcore, will also play 5 tracks from their upcoming album. Throughout the concert, the bands' performances will be accompanied by projected live gameplays.

  "Afterparty" concept, which we will happen between 00.00 and 04.00, is entrusted to the hands of three DJs. DJ BODY PISTOL, RASENV1RUS and RANDOM INPUT will offer us breakcore, grindcore, hardcore, IDM selections.

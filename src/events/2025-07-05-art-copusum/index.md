  As humanity, we have been throwing garbage since civilization was founded. With the discovery and widespread use of petroleum-based, plastic and similar materials, it has been discovered that the planet we live in is a giant garbage bin. We take the disposable products we use out of their disposable bags and leave them in our disposable world. We have become disposable people.

  This Saturday, there will be a commotion in Izmir where we celebrate the adoption of garbage as a way of life. Come and let's tear the bag of this new event together.

  Prepared by Kuduruk, Peaceful Chaos, Random Input, Balgum, Böcek and Ox, the event featured a market on the ground, and a stage with music and performances on the upper floor.

  In the garden, we set up an art market where artists from different disciplines participated with their works, and an area where we exchanged unused items, clothes, jewelry, handicrafts, tools and devices.

  Upstairs, Ox, Random Input and Balgum played DJ sets, while Çöpten Çıkan performed a clown show and Böcek performed a live painting.

  ##### open call for **∆RT Market**

  If you want to take part in the art market, where artists from different disciplines can participate with all kinds of styles and works, contact _kuduruk_.
  !! The quota for the art market is limited!!

  ##### call for **B∆RTER**

  We will create a free area where you can bring and circulate your unused items, clothes, jewelry, handicrafts, tools and devices, whatever you want to trade. You can leave what you want to share here, and you can take what you want from what is left. You can display the things you want to trade and exchange them for other things. If you have questions about the barter market, you can contact _peaceful_chaos_. (灬º‿º灬) ♡

  Bu Cumartesi, İzmir'de, çöpün bir yaşam biçimi olarak bu kadar benimsenmesini kutladığımız bir keşmekeş yaşanacak. Gelin hazırladığımız bu yepisyeni etkinliğin poşetini beraber yırtalım.

  Alt katta ve bahçede farklı disiplinlerden sanatçıların eserliyle katıldığı bir sanat pazarı ve kullanmadığın eşyaları, kıyafetleri, takıları, el işlerini, alet ve cihazları, takas ettiği bir alan oluşturduk.

  Üst katta sahnede Ox, Random Input ve Balgum DJ setlerini çalarken, Çöpten Çıkan palyaço gösterisini, Böcek ise live painting performansını sergiledi.

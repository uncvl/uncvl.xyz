bio_en: |-
  E. Berhan Yıldız, known as Fosil, is an Istanbul-based sound designer, producer, DJ, and the founder of Fossss Music. With over eight years of experience as an organizer, he has performed at prominent events like Sónar Festival and across diverse international cities including Tbilisi, Tokyo, Bangkok, Belgrade, Nagoya, and Da Nang. Since 2015, his practice has been defined by an unconventional sonic language—blending emotional textures, cartoon samples, and atonal textures with off-grid rhythms and broken groove structures. He is also a member of the UK-based label Scuffed Recordings.
